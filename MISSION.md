# Mission and vision

## Mission

Build an open, extensible platform that helps people connect to laboratory hardware, capture and understand signals, and work with instruments. Document what has been verified and make it easier for contributors to add devices, communication methods, analysis tools, and platforms.

## Vision

A flexible laboratory workspace where students, makers, engineers, and researchers can use USB- and Ethernet-connected hardware through a consistent interface, including logic analyzers and rack instruments using SCPI, inspect their own data, and improve the tools together. We aspire to reach Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, and iOS where platform capabilities and hardware access permit.

## Principles

- **Open collaboration:** Make development and decisions approachable, and welcome contributions of different kinds.
- **Extensibility:** Design clear boundaries for devices, capture, analysis, and interfaces so the platform can grow beyond its first hardware family.
- **Trust through evidence:** Distinguish tested behavior from assumptions, document limitations, and share reproducible findings.
- **Respect for users and sources:** Keep users in control of their data and follow the project's clean-room contribution rules.
- **Practical access:** Make the project useful without specialized hardware through demo sources, and improve accessibility across supported platforms.

These are goals, not claims that all devices or platforms are supported today. See the [README](README.md) for current capabilities and limitations.
