About LogicView

LogicView is an open-source workspace for capturing and exploring signals. It brings logic analysis, waveform measurements, and protocol decoding together, with demo devices for exploring without hardware.

E2ForLife

E2ForLife is a small organization passionate about engineering, embedded systems, firmware, and education.

We build tools like LogicView to make working with technology more approachable, useful, and enjoyable. Our goal is to help people learn, experiment, solve problems, and turn ideas into working projects.

We believe in the maker lifestyle: stay curious, keep building, share what you learn, and never stop exploring how things work.
